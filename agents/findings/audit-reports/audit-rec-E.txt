1. Options getter/setter numbering (CONFIRMED): the getter (slot 71) maps 0 a7[0], 1 bGimmes, 2 a0[0], 3 a0[4], 4 a7[1], 5 a24[6], 6 a24[4], 7 a0[1], 8 a24[1]. The setter (slot 72) maps 0 byte 7 (=a7[0], via fn_8002EBA4, applied to 4 controllers), 1 bGimmes, 2 a0[0], 3 a0[4], 4 a7[1], 5 a0[1]. So a0[1] is getter 7 but setter 5, and getter 5/6/8 have no setter here. The a0[4] remaps round-trip (getter 0..5 -> 2,3,4,5,6,1; setter 1..6 -> 5,0..4). An out-of-range index (or a0[4] > 5) leaves pResult unset. This is EA's command numbering, not a bug we can see. Name the two index sets separately in the later pass.
