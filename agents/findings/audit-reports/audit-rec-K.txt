- fn_800D256C (called by 8010A3C8): confirmed. It counts 1900 as a leap year. This is already marked as an EA bug in Calendar.c, and the day numbers depend on it. The declaration comment at line 16 is accurate.
- 801093B4: confirmed. Lock kind 2 gives "Game Boy(R) Advance Link Required" (EA text in fn_8010651C; the string has the \xAE registered mark). Other lock kinds get the asset's CR_S text n110. The string is left unchanged when there is none or when i is out of range.
